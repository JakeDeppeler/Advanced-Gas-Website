# Suburb notes worksheet

Fill in what you know. Skip what you don't. Anything left blank
stays as it is, so this is safe to do in bits.

When it's filled in:

```
node scripts/suburb-notes.mjs import docs/suburb-notes.md
```

---

## The rules

**Testimonials must be real.** A real customer, a real job, and
either a review they've already left publicly or a customer who has
said yes. Made-up quotes are fake reviews. Don't, and I won't
write them for you either. If a suburb has no real quote, leave it
blank; the page works without one.

**Everything else should be specific enough that a competitor
couldn't guess it.** "Older homes, lots of gas heating" is worth
nothing, because it's true of forty suburbs and Google can see
that. "The Rinnai tanks that went in with the Cameron Park estate
build are all hitting fifteen years at once" is worth a lot,
because only someone who has been on those streets knows it.

**Write how you talk.** This gets published close to verbatim.

---

## The format

```
# suburb-slug
why: One paragraph. Why we're actually embedded here. Jobs done,
     sponsorships, who lives here, how long we've worked it.
estates: The estates, streets or precincts you know by name.
problem: One thing that goes wrong here, and why.
problem: Another. Three or four is plenty.
quote: What they said | Their name, Suburb | What the job was
```

`problem:` can repeat. Everything else appears once. A line can run
as long as you like; wrap it if it helps you read it.
